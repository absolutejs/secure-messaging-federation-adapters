# Security policy

Experimental, unaudited, and not a completed MIMI implementation. Do not use the
profile ID as evidence of interoperability or standards conformance.

Require the exact draft advertisement, explicit opt-in, mutually authenticated
TLS, the federation core's bilateral transcript confirmation, and strict E2EE.
Do not downgrade to managed recovery, plaintext, another revision, or an
unadvertised content/capability set.
